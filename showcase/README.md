# Showcase

A pair of sample decks per theme, one in Chinese and one in English, written to look their best. Each sample is a deck project: a `deck.spec.json` that binds the theme, plus one file per page under `pages/`.

| theme | sample |
| --- | --- |

A tea chain's strategy team takes listed tea chains' H1 2026 results and industry data to its leadership and asks to make same-store growth the first target for 2027. 12 pages, real public data with a source on every data page. [Chinese](brief/zh/) and [English](brief/en/). |

[Chinese](swiss/zh/) and [English](swiss/en/). |

Render a sample from the repository root:

```bash
pnpm build
node dist/cli.js render showcase/brief/en -o out/brief-en.pptx
node dist/cli.js preview showcase/brief/en -o out/brief-en
node dist/cli.js audit showcase/brief/en
```

Each theme has a pair of samples on the same topic, one in Chinese and one in English. The figures come from public filings, industry reports and regulators, and every data page names its source. The street photo in brief's sample, the port photo in bulletin's, the storage photo in swiss's, the data center photo in ledger's, the factory photo in vermilion's and the seven photographs and the dashboard in terminal's, the seven photographs in memo's, the five in clinic's, the eight in almanac's and the six in homeroom's are AI-generated, and their pages say so. The page files show how to mark the one thing each page is about: `**…**` in text and on a figure, `emphasis` on waterfall bars, a chart series, a roadmap item, a numbered card and a gantt stretch, `recommended` on a comparison column, and `highlight` on a table row or a milestone. bulletin's sample also shows how to say what a number is: `status: "forecast"` and `"target"` on a chart point, `changes` for a bracket between two bars, and `lane` to run a timeline on two tracks. swiss's sample marks the one bar a page is about with `emphasis` on that chart point, draws one whole as a share bar (a `stacked` chart with `direction: "horizontal"` and one category, its marked run of parts totalled under it), and sets a page's figures as `kpi_cards` beside a chart, under a statement and under a photograph. ledger's sample sets a `kicker` over the cover's and the ending's titles, a ticker of `kpi_cards` on the cover, a dot plot of guidance moves (a `dumbbell` chart), a timeline over a note written as "label: text", the ending's next steps as "label: text" bullets under the decision, and the ending's `footnote` as a disclaimer. vermilion's sample tags the rows of a comparison and a `from_to` with what changed (`tag`, `tag_column`, a `quiet` tag for nothing changed) and marks the row the page is about with `emphasis`, names the column of a `from_to`'s measures (`label_column`), tags a figure (`tag` on a `kpi_cards` item), marks a target range across a line chart (`bands`), gives each completion rate its amounts (`detail` on `progress_donuts`), lists ten tasks as `numbered_cards`, and sets a plan's statement in an `insight_panel` beside its targets. terminal's sample sets a status line on the cover (`kicker` over a photograph), quotes postmortems as a `code` block with a `title` and `highlight_lines`, colours milestones, figures and log lines by the kind of news they are (`tone`), puts a symbol on milestones, roadmap phases, tree branches, table rows and picture captions (`icon`), names the incident behind each cause (`tag` on `icon_cards`), writes durations as a console prints them (`2h52m`), marks a matrix cell with ✓, ✕ or —, names a tree's second column (`children_column`), and ends on a checklist drawn from a `timeline`. memo's sample writes the cover's header lines and the sign-off's lines as `fields` and stamps both pages (`stamp`), sets each page's section in the margin with `kicker`, pastes its photographs in as numbered exhibits, says which way a bar is good or bad news with `tone` on chart series, tags each row of a table with the kind of source it comes from (`tag`), says who the recommended option is for (`recommended_label`), works a sum as "label: working" bullets over its answer, writes a rota as a table of blank and 「休」 cells, and closes on stop conditions written "kind: measure". clinic's sample names the kind of source behind a figure (`evidence` on a `tag`: a trial, a label, a company's figures, the press, a draft, an official file), tags a whole page with the evidence it rests on (`tag` on the page) and a chart with its own (`tag` on `chart`), sets each page's section beside the heartbeat with `kicker`, writes each trial against its placebo as a marked series and a plain one, writes hazard ratios in a table as "0.80（0.72 至 0.90）", pairs each drug's row with its placebo's in a table of rates, marks the steps that can stop a review (`tone: "danger"` on `steps`), sets each option's proposal as a tag on its comparison row that says how settled it is (`settled`, `quiet`), puts a symbol on numbered cards, steps, table rows and a panel (`icon`), names a table's label column (`label_column`), and ends on a ballot (`ballot` with `choices` and a `signature`) over the submission's `fields`. almanac's sample lights the years each page is about on a strip of years (`years` on the page), says what a figure or a rule rests on with `basis` on a `tag` (a provision of law, an estimate at a stated assumption, a figure still pending, a rule only proposed) on figures, a chart, a callout, a milestone and the page itself, divides a timeline into named spans (`periods`, a law and a proposal), gives each milestone a tag and a source, titles and tags a `callout`, notes a waterfall bar and a chart bar (`note`), draws a reference value across bars (`reference`), writes a share bar's marked run in its own words (`emphasis_label`), marks a roadmap row's budget line as pending (`basis` on a row), sets a formula as `code` with `language: "formula"` beside "symbol: meaning" bullets, and draws the cover's scale of years from a `timeline` of years. homeroom's sample names the course a class runs through (`course` on the deck, quizzes marked) and the stage each page is in (`stage`), sets each page's step with `kicker`, lays out the class by the minute (`duration`, `duration_unit`, `checkpoint` and `points` on a `roadmap`), labels each study with its kind (`evidence`, a working paper as `preprint`), draws a value known only as a range (`upper` on a chart point), sets each quiz as a `ballot` over its cases and each answer as a title written "case: verdict" with a `tone`, colours information grades (`tone` on pyramid levels), cites the law behind each rule (`basis: "law"`), marks the word to remember on the board (`highlight`), and ends on homework written as `numbered_cards` with a `stamp`.
