A content page whose composition cannot hold its component in full now hands the page to a plain full-width sheet instead of losing content, as long as the sheet holds everything. The decision is made by rendering the real content path for both boxes and asking whether anything was dropped, so width, height and each component's own squeezing rules all count. Seventeen compositions take part, the handed-over page keeps its theme motif, branding and accent, and the cross-language overflow list is empty. Also fixed: the data faces of `brief` and `runway` painted two chart series in the same colour when reserving the theme accent, so two-series charts on those themes now read as two.
