Three new faces for swiss's report pages. `grid-sheet` heads every content page with the chapter's number and name, a black bold claim across the full width over a heavy rule, and hands the body to the grid compositions. `grid-statement` sets the deck's conclusion large over the figures it rests on, and `grid-figure` sets one figure very large with two supporting figures beside it. `image-top` takes a `band` parameter, and `"grid"` sets the claim on a black rule under the photograph with the figures in columns below.
