crayon is redrawn as a box of crayons on drawing paper, to a kindergarten parents' meeting. Every content page wears a rounded capsule at the top left in the crayon of the section it sits in (the page's `kicker`): a deck's sections take sky, grass green, tangerine, red and purple in the order they first appear, a chapter names its section by its heading, and the capsule carries the symbol the deck's own contents gives that section. The claim is set in a heavy rounded hand on one line across the page, broken at a comma or a colon only when it has to be, with a stroke of crayon in the section's colour under it. Cards are rounded and outlined twice, as if traced by hand. A sun and two stars sit in the top right corner, and the page number stands in a pale disc of the section's crayon beside the deck's name and term. A content page over a photograph lays the paper over it from the left. The cover sets the occasion in a sky capsule, the title large with a tangerine stroke, the subtitle and the term beside the page's photograph in a crayon frame. A chapter opens on its number on a tilted block of its section's crayon beside a framed photograph. The close sets the next meeting, a contact card for each way to reach the school (an `icon_cards`, or a list whose items read 「名字：说明」) and the line to remember on a tangerine pill, over a veiled photograph. crayon now offers the `data` kind, and its colours gain the five section crayons (`accentPool`) and a burnt orange for words the tangerine has to carry (`emphasisInk`). If you copied crayon into your own theme file, its menu still names the old faces and keeps working as before.
