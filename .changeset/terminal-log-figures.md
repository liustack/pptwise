terminal draws a timeline beside plain figures as its incident log. A `process` page of a timeline and a `kpi_cards` whose values are not lengths of time (「16 个月」, 「4 位」) used to fall to the ordinary renderer, a horizontal timeline with its labels crowded into narrow cells. The log now sets those figures in the figure panels its board already uses beside the ranked bar chart: the label in mono, the figure in bold mono, the note under it, the marked figure on the mark's tint. Durations still draw to scale as before.
