The statement layout on ink, ledger, luxe and memo no longer cuts a claim too long for its one or two lines with no mark, and the pull-quote layout on ink, journal, ledger, luxe, memo, stage and thesis no longer runs a long heading or subheading line over the quote off the page. On ink, a Chinese quote's context and attribution columns no longer run past the foot of the page. Each of these pages now goes to the shared statement or pull-quote layout, which sets the line whole or marks what it cuts. Pages whose lines already fit look exactly as before.
