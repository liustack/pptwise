The marked words of a `verdict_banner` read on the page. They take the verdict's tone, and on the light themes' paper the raw amber of a warning came out between 2.42:1 and 2.93:1 and the green of a positive verdict between 2.79:1 and just under 3:1 on six of them, short of the 3:1 their size needs. The run is now darkened just far enough to clear that floor and keeps its hue, so it still reads as the verdict's tone. The mark and the icon keep the tone as it was, and themes where the tone already reads are unchanged.
