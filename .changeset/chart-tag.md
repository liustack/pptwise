A chart can carry a `tag`, a few words over it saying what kind of figures it draws, such as 企业口径 or "Company figures", with an optional `evidence` kind that colours it by its source. The ordinary chart sets it at the start of its legend row, the legend right-aligned beside it. Hand-set plots with no place for it hand the page to the ordinary chart. A share bar refuses one.
