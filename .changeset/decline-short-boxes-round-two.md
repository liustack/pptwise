A hub and spoke drawing, sankey diagram, five forces cross or set of numbered cards given less height than its smallest complete form now declines the box, marking the whole component as dropped, instead of setting words that stop at their minimum size below the box. A hub and spoke drawing still scales down into a short box as long as its capsules and hub stay tall enough for their words. A sankey node label left off for want of room beside its node is now declared dropped rather than left out silently. Components given the height they ask for render exactly as before.
