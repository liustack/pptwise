Positioning maps keep each subject's name inside its own quadrant and clear of both axis lines, so a name near the middle of the map is no longer drawn across an axis. When a crowded corner leaves a name nowhere to go, earlier names move to make room before any name is left off. An axis end name moves to the other side of its axis when a subject's dot sits on it. People cards wrap a long name or affiliation onto a second line instead of cutting it short, and a title gets a third line. A roster too wide for its panel now moves to a wider layout rather than print part of a name. Business model canvas items wrap under their bullet instead of being cut at the end of the first line.
