A point of a `line` chart can carry a few words (`data[].note`), such as "Low" on its lowest year or "Rebound" on the year it recovers. The ordinary line prints an interior point's note with its value, under a point lower than its neighbours and over any other, and by the first or last point prints the note beside the value its gutter already carries.
