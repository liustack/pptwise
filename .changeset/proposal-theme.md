A new built-in theme, `proposal`, sets every page as a client proposal in a ring binder: white paper with sand cards, petrol figures and one brick-red mark on what the page lands on, and the binder's tabs down the right edge naming the deck's sections (`course.stages`) with the page's own section lit. Its cover sets a white proposal page beside the deck's photograph, with the date, a chip naming who the proposal is for, the title and three figures. Its chapter numbers the part huge in brick red over the photograph and lists the questions the part answers. Its ending sets the decisions the client is asked to make as cards with a box for each choice, and a brick-red button in the author's own words. The deck's label stands at the top left of every content page and the page number at the bottom right. `pptwise themes` now lists 25 presets, and `sales` joins the occasions a theme can serve.
