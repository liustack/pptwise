On a page painted a mid-tone `background` color, about as light as `#777777` or `#6B7B8C`, a card a step darker than the page left its text under the 4.5:1 body text needs on most dark themes: a KPI label read at 4.0:1 on `rally`. Such a card now takes its step lighter instead, where the text reads, and the step itself stays the same size. On `#777777`, the `low-contrast` findings across every component on every theme fall from 966 to 46. Pages that already read render exactly as before.
