Curly quotation marks in an English deck no longer open a wide gap in PowerPoint. Microsoft YaHei sets ‘ ’ “ ” as full-width Chinese marks, and PowerPoint drew an English sentence's quotes from it with a space the width of a Chinese character beside each one. A deck not written in Chinese now sets its YaHei text in Microsoft YaHei UI, the same font with Western quotation marks, and keeps YaHei for its Chinese characters. A Chinese deck is unchanged.
