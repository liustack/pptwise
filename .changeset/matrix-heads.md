A `matrix` can name its columns (`columns`) and its rows (`rows`, each with an optional icon), carry a short name over it (`title`), and leave a cell empty (`items[].empty`), such as a gap in the evidence. The ordinary grid names the columns over the cells and the rows at their left, sets the title over it as a table does, and draws an empty cell as a dashed outline with its words in the middle, in the accent with `tone: "accent"`.
