Every chart in a deck now prints its figures one way. The language of a chart's figures and the words around them (「个百分点」 or "pts", 「预测」 or "forecast") comes from the deck's headings instead of the chart's own labels, so a Chinese deck's chart of quarters no longer prints its figures the English way beside a chart of company names. A Chinese deck groups four-digit figures (「3,291」) when its author writes four-digit figures grouped anywhere in the deck, and otherwise leaves them whole (「3291」) as before. Five digits and more are grouped either way.
