A `data_table` row can carry a `tag`, a word or two saying what kind of row it is (倡导, 独立, Independent), printed as a small tag at the start of the row's last cell. The ordinary table draws it, outlined in the accent, grey when `quiet`, filled on the highlighted row. Compositions with no place for it hand the page to the ordinary table.
