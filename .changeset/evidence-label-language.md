The label over an evidence page's card follows the deck's language on almanac, brief, museum, rally and vermilion. An English deck used to print a Chinese word there, such as 「案卷 · 13」 on vermilion. It now prints "File · 13", "Sample 13", "Evidence 13", "Exhibit № 13" or "Measured · 13".
