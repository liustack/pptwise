Four new faces drawn from terminal's 2026-10 board. `console-sheet` sets every ordinary page as an incident console: a mono line at the top left that says where the page sits (`01 / Review · P04`), the claim bold across the page over a hairline, the body handed to the compositions in the `console` setting, and a mono source line. Seven compositions are new and drawn only in that setting: `cards` sets findings as HUD cards with icons and tags, `listing` sets a code block as a terminal window with its marked lines, `log` sets a timeline as an incident log with a dot per tone and a column beside it, `span` sets lengths of time to scale, `plates` sets pictures over their figures, `paths` sets an issue tree as failure points beside their fixes, and `screen` sets a browser screenshot beside its log lines. `console-cover` and `console-chapter` draw their own pages over a photograph, darkened from the left, the chapter page listing its chapter's pages as a directory, and `console-ending` sets the decision over a checklist of dated to-dos from a timeline or from bullets. A cover or chapter face can now say it draws over its own photograph, so the page keeps its face, its kicker and its components instead of going to the shared photo page.
