A bar or line chart can keep a place for a category nobody published a value for (`gaps`), such as a year a survey skipped: `[{ "after": "2017", "x": "2018", "label": "Not published" }]`. The ordinary bar chart draws a dashed outline there, about as tall as its bars run, with the label over it. The ordinary line breaks there and sets the label over the axis.
