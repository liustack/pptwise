A comparison row can say what happened to it. `rows[].tag` prints a few words, such as "Now a range" or "Unchanged", as a small label after the row's cells, under the header `tag_column`. `rows[].emphasis` marks the one row the page is about: it sits on a pale tint and its tag fills in the theme's emphasis colour. A tag with `quiet: true`, one that says nothing changed, steps back in a grey outline, and any other tag is outlined in the accent. Every theme sets them in its ordinary table.
