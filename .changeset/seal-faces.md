Two new content faces and a new `image-split` column, drawn from vermilion's 2026-10 board. `seal-sheet` sets every ordinary page as an official document: the claim centred and bold in the brand colour over a short accent bar, the body handed to the compositions in the `seal` setting, and the source at 14px at the foot. Its menu parameter `cards` sets numbered cards as rows (the default) or as panels (`"tiles"`). Five compositions are new and drawn only in that setting: `roster` sets six to ten short items in two columns of numbered cells, `scores` sets a scorecard as an open table with each verdict as a tag, `targets` sets an `insight_panel` statement in a reversed block beside a `from_to` table of targets, `trend` sets one line over its value axis with a marked range behind it, and `rings` sets completion rates as large rings with the amounts behind them. `seal-figure` sets one figure at 240px with its tag and note, and up to three supporting figures beside it. The `seal` column of `image-split` lays the photograph down one side edge to edge, with the claim and a column of figures beside it. A theme names them in its menu like any other face.
