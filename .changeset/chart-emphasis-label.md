A share bar can carry an `emphasis_label`: the line it states for its marked parts in the author's own words, such as what they add up to ("第 73 章制品 €93.5 亿，占 69.5%"). The ordinary share bar sets it where the run's computed total would stand, and leaves the bar undrawn rather than cut a line wider than the bar. Any chart that is not a share bar with a marked series refuses it. The shared hand-set plots leave such a chart to the ordinary one.
