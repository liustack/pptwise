A comparison table, chevron process, KPI card row, progress donut row or row of side-by-side steps that is given less height than its wrapped text needs now takes wrapped lines back, keeping each first line and marking the cut, instead of drawing its last lines below the box without saying so. A hub and spoke drawing given less height than it measures now scales down into that height. Drawings given the height they ask for render exactly as before.
