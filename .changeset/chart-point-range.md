A bar on its side can carry a value known only as a range: `upper` on a point is its high end, `y` its low end, such as 60 to 70 for two groups that scored about 60% and 70%. The bar is drawn solid to its low end and dashed on to its high end over a pale tint, and its label names both ends (「60 至 70」, "60–70"). validate refuses `upper` off a horizontal bar chart, on a value below zero, under its own `y`, or beside a `status`.
