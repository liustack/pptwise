A headline figure can carry a `tag`, a few words saying what it is, such as "Binding" or "Estimate". The ordinary cards print it as a small label on a row under the figure's label, filled in the emphasis colour on the figure the page marks with `**…**` and outlined on the others. A layout that sets figures by hand with no place for a tag hands the page to the ordinary cards.
