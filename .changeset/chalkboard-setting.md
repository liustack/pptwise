The shared compositions gain a `chalkboard` setting: a night class at a green board, words in chalk white, what is quieter in a wiped chalk grey, and one stroke of yellow chalk drawn by hand under the thing to remember. Sixteen compositions come with it: `agenda` (what tonight covers in boxes of chalk), `confluence` (two paths meeting in one result beside a photograph, from a `concept_equation`), `braces` (a formula with a brace under each term its "term：note" bullets explain), `boughs` (a decision tree in chalk with the recommended answer ringed), `factors` (things beside their factors, written after a multiplication sign in `sub`), `subtractions` (what is taken off as a row of minus signs), `flashcards` (two rows of cards, the tagged ones ringed), `risers` (a rate table as a staircase), `givens` (an example's givens beside its photographs), `derivation` (a sum worked line by line with its equals signs in a column), `cascade` (a waterfall as chalk bars, the marked step dashed in yellow), `exercises` (questions lettered in rings with a line to answer on), `solutions` (the answers worked in columns), `pitfalls` (mistakes struck with a cross, from `row_cards` marked `tone: "danger"`), `strikeout` (figures with the misquoted one struck out, from a warning `callout` titled by it) and `chronology` (dates at their true distance with the window ahead boxed). Any face can offer them, and lecture's content pages use them.
