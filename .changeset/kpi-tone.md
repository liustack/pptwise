A `kpi_cards` item can carry a `tone` (`danger`, `warning` or `success`) that says what kind of news the figure is. The ordinary card paints its icon and its label in the theme's colour for the tone. Faces that set figures by hand and have no place for a tone hand the page to the ordinary cards, as they do a tagged figure.
