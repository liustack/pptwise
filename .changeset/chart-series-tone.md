A chart series can say what kind of news it is: `tone` ("success", "danger", "warning") draws the series and its legend swatch in the colour every theme keeps for that kind of news, on bar, line, area, scatter, stacked, percent_stacked and combo charts. A percent_stacked chart of who got better, who stayed the same and who got worse no longer hands the worse share whatever palette colour came second. Hand-set plots that cannot paint a tone hand the page to the ordinary chart.
