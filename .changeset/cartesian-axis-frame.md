Cartesian charts (bar, line, area, scatter) draw a left+bottom axis with ticks outside the plot. Axis titles sit under the drawing on one line: y-name with ↑, then x-name with →. Heatmap and matrix use the same pair under the grid. Scatter domains pad so points sit in the middle of the frame, not against the edge. Optional `axes.x_unit` / `axes.y_unit` print on the ticks. Bubble pages need a footnote that says what bubble area means.
