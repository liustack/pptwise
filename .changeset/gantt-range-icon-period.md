A `gantt` can run its axis over a stretch of its own (`range`, such as a whole 18-month plan whose bars cover parts of it), and a row can carry an `icon` and a `period` (「第 16 至 18 个月」, "Months 16 to 18"). The ordinary gantt runs its axis over the range, sets the icon before the row's label and the period under it. Validate refuses a bar outside the range. Hand-set gantts hand such a page to the ordinary gantt.
