A waterfall now prints a space between a figure and its unit, 「382.1 万辆」 and "3.82 million units" instead of 「382.1万辆」. A percent sign or a Latin magnitude (k, m, bn) stays glued to its figure, "12%" and "2m", on a waterfall and on a chart axis alike, so an English axis no longer prints "2 m", which reads as two metres.
