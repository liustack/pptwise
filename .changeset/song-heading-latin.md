memo's headings set their Western text and figures in Times New Roman and their Chinese in SimSun. A font stack that names a Latin face before a Chinese one now pairs them: the preview draws each script from its own face, and the export writes the Chinese face into each run's East Asian slot. memo's English headings used to print on SimSun's half-width grid in PowerPoint while the preview showed a proportional serif. Times New Roman is measured from its own advance widths.
