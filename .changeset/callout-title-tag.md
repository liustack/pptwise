A callout can carry a `title`, a short bold line over its text naming what the note is about (such as 谁付 or Who pays), and a `tag`, a few words under its text that say what the note rests on (such as 企业口径 · 据报道, with a `basis` or an `evidence` kind). The ordinary callout draws both inside its panel and declares a tag wider than its text dropped. The shared hand-set compositions leave such a page to the ordinary callout.
