Every theme, page kind and component now carries a design story: a name, what it is and what it borrows from the real world, when to choose it, who speaks and who listens, and when not to use it. The stories live next to their definitions (`story` on a theme file, `KIND_STORIES`, each component module) and are checked in tests for length, complete sentences, and a naming rule: a name says how a deck speaks, never which industry it is for. The review gallery shows each story as a design card under its theme section and component detail, with a Chinese translation for all 74 entries. Public theme files are held to the same rule, so an id or label that names an industry is refused with the offending word.
