A timeline milestone can carry an `icon`, drawn in a ring on the axis where its dot would be, and a `tone`: `danger` for something that broke, `warning` for something still being handled, `success` for something that recovered. The ordinary timeline paints the dot or the ring in the theme's own colour for the tone. Compositions that have no place for either hand the page to the ordinary timeline.
