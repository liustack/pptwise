Vermilion has a new look, the page of an official document. Points, list, comparison, process, data, evidence and hierarchy pages use `seal-sheet`: the claim centred in red, items numbered 一、二、三 in small red squares, open tables under a red rule, red spent once a page and gold only for rules, bars and outlines. Fact pages use `seal-figure`, and vermilion offers photo pages for the first time, with `image-split`'s `seal` column. The chart palette puts two warm greys after the red, so data nobody marked steps back. If you copied vermilion into your own theme file, its menu still names the old faces and keeps working as before.
