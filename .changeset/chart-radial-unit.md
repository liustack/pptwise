A pie, a donut and a funnel print the value axis's unit (`axes.y_unit`) after every value they name (「1200 个」, "30%"), and a donut after its centre total. These charts have no axis, so a `y_unit` alone no longer draws the `chart_axes_ignored` warning; their other axis settings still do.
