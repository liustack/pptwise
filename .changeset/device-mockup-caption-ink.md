A `device_mockup` caption sets its words in an ink its band can read, and the band under it is opaque, the way a picture's caption band already was. On dark themes whose primary is a dark block colour the caption used to sit between 1.02:1 and 1.25:1 (rally, luxe, ledger, stage, terminal, museum, lecture), and crayon's at 3.96:1.
