A subheading on nine cover, chapter and ending layouts that no built-in theme uses is now refused by validate instead of disappearing from the slide. A theme file can put any layout on its menu, and these nine never drew a subheading: the Title Plate and Open Pledge covers, the Broadsheet Numeral, Solo Title, Color Block and Gilt Ordinal chapters, and the Bare Close, Lead Rule and Gilt Accent endings. Each refusal names the layout and says where the words can go, and points at the spec page's `summary`, which is where a deck project's subheading comes from.
