The shared compositions take an eighth setting, `dossier`: a clinical assessment file, the way a committee reads a submission. Proposals are numbered on cards under the page's section (「提议 1」), with their icons and where their evidence is, the one the page argues for filled with the mark. A comparison sets each option's proposal as a capsule that says how settled it is: a settled yes filled, a conditional yes outlined in the mark, an open no or a deferral outlined in grey, a settled no filled in a quiet grey. Approvals run on two lanes and rules sit on cards two by two. The setting's capsules name the kind of source each figure rests on in its own ink, and what a result is read against is drawn in outline.
