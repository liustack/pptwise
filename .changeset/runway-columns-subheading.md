The Runway Columns statement layout, which no built-in theme's menu uses, now prints a page's subheading as a muted line between the claim's bar and the rule over the columns, in the type it sets the columns' text and the source line in. It used to leave the subheading off the slide without saying so. A page without a subheading looks as before.
