A `steps` badge sets its number in an ink its disc can read. The disc is painted in the page colour and the number used to take the chevron's fill, so on themes whose primary is a dark block colour it nearly vanished: rally, luxe, ledger, stage, terminal, museum and lecture came out between 1.05:1 and 1.38:1, and crayon at 3.78:1. Themes whose primary already reads on the page keep it.
