A bar chart whose series cover different categories now centres each bar on its category. Before, each bar kept the place its series would have had in a full group and stood off to one side of the name printed under it. Writing each group of categories as its own series, such as full years beside half-years, is now the way to colour two groups apart, with no zeros or stacking needed.
