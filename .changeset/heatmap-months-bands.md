A `heatmap` runs up to twelve columns, a year of months, and can mark a run of them (`bands`, such as the season a plan is built around): the ordinary heat grid frames the run across every row in a dashed outline of the accent and names it under the grid. Validate refuses a run that names a column the grid does not have, runs backwards, or shares a column with another.
