luxe is redrawn as a gilt invitation. Every content page sits on a card with a fine frame 24px in from its edge, the chapter it belongs to small and centred at the top between two short gold rules (the page's `kicker`, now accepted on every content page), the claim centred in a gold serif on one line and broken at a comma or a colon only when it has to be, a gold diamond under its last line wherever that ends, the source at the foot, the occasion and the date at the bottom left (the deck's `organization` and the footer's `label`) and the page number struck as a small gold hallmark at the bottom right. Gold goes to lines, letters and the one figure a page is about, never to a solid card. Its cover sets the invitation in a double gilt frame beside the page's photograph, its chapter pages open each part under a veil of black with the part's Roman numeral in gold, and its ending signs the card with the page's `subheading` as the house's sign-off. A content page with a `stamp` is a reply card, the stamp standing down its torn stub. The heading face is now Times New Roman with SimSun, so large figures and English titles are set in a Latin serif and an English title's quotation marks are Western. Its statement and quotation pages keep their layout inside the new frame. If you copied luxe into your own theme file, its menu still names the old faces and keeps working as before.
