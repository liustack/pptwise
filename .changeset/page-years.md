A page can carry `years`: the run of years a deck follows (`from`, `to`) and the years this page is about (`marked`), drawn as a strip of years in the running head with the page's own years lit. At most 13 years, every marked year inside the run. Only a face that declares a place for it draws it, and validate refuses it on any other, naming the face. Page files may fill it.
