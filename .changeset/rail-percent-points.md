The figure column beside a brief trend chart now states a change in percentage points when the chart's axis is in percent: 80.1% to 91.0% reads "+10.9 pts", or 「+10.9 个百分点」 on a chart written in Chinese. It used to print the relative change, "+14%". Other units still show the relative change.
