On DeepSeek Harness the pptwise cards now look like DeepSeek Harness's own cards and follow its light and dark themes, in the user's language. Full screen opens in the harness's right sidebar, which can widen to the whole window with its own button, and the keyboard is in the deck from the moment it opens: ←/→, PageUp/PageDown and Space turn pages, Home and End jump, Esc closes, the left and right edges of the page and the strip of pages below it are clickable, a sideways trackpad swipe turns a page, and "Page N of M" shows where you are. Clicking a page in the chat opens it on that page. The draft card marks a changed page with a small tag under it, and "Edit this page" appears when you point at a page. Comparing with the last version opens in the sidebar too.
