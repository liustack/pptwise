A tag can say what its figure or rule rests on: `basis` is `law` (a provision in force, which the tag cites), `estimate` (a figure worked out under a stated assumption, not published and not a forecast), `pending` (a figure or item still to be confirmed or filled in) or `proposal` (a rule proposed or still negotiated, not yet law). The ordinary tag outlines the law in the primary, an estimate and a proposal in the accent and a pending figure in the muted ink, and dashes the three that are not settled. A basis beside `settled` or `evidence` is refused, since each says the same a second time.
