homeroom is redrawn as one lesson. Its cover sets a blackboard beside the classroom photograph with the occasion, the title over a wavy line of the correcting pen and the lesson's facts as pills; its chapter page lays each part of the lesson on a band of board, the part's name boxed in the author's own words, with what the part covers on three cards; its ending sets the homework on ruled paper with a sticky note and a stamp, the title always drawn and up to five tasks. Every content page names its step at the top left (`kicker`), lights its stage on the deck's course strip at the top right (`course`, `stage`), sets its claim over the pen's wavy line and hands its body to fourteen new compositions in a lesson setting: goals with boxes to tick, a class laid out by the minute, studies with the kind of study in a pill, the weaker group against the stronger, two studies with their charts, expectations against a measurement, a quiz with a box for each choice and its answers stamped, cases, a ranking with one item broken down, house rules with their grounds, levels from the most guarded down, methods under their photographs and the recap on a blackboard. The footer is homeroom's own folio, the office and the course at the left and 「N / M」 at the right.
