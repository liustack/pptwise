An English heading with two sentences now breaks between them when the first fits on its line. swiss's statement read "Clean power met all new" over "demand. Not settled yet." and now reads "Clean power met all new demand." over "Not settled yet.", the way a Chinese heading breaks after its comma. The break never sets a line wider than the plain wrap would, so the heading keeps its size, and the lines the break leaves are evened. A last sentence of one word does not get a line to itself, and a full stop in an initialism such as "U.S." is not read as a sentence end. Balanced quotes follow the same rule.
