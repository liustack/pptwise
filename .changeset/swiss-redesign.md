Swiss has a new look. Every page carries an 8px red bar along the top, and the grey ticks on the cover are gone. Content pages open with the chapter's number in red and its name, a black bold claim across the full width and a heavy black rule, with no title band and no section badge. Data is black, steps back in grey, and red marks the one thing a page is about. Points, list, comparison, process, data, evidence and hierarchy pages use `grid-sheet`, statement pages `grid-statement`, fact pages `grid-figure`, and photo pages `image-top` with its grid band. The cover, chapter and ending pages are redrawn, and a chapter page now lists the pages it holds. If you copied swiss into your own theme file, its menu still names the old faces and keeps working as before.
