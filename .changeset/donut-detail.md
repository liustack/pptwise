A completion ring can say what its rate is made of and which ring the page is about. `progress_donuts.items[].detail` prints the amounts behind a rate, such as "1.18 of 1.3 trillion", on a line under its label and before its source, and `items[].emphasis` sets the one rate the page is about, its ring, figure and label, in the theme's emphasis colour.
