A headline figure's unit is set a space after the number, the way the rest of a deck writes it: "2 months" and 「936.70 元」, no longer "2months" and 「936.70元」. A percent sign, a Latin magnitude ("2m", "3.4bn") and a multiplication sign stay glued to their figure. The space comes out of the unit's own room, so the number keeps the size it had.
