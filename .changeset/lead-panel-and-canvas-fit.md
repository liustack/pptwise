On the page with a wide lead column and two framed panels, the lead column now goes to the first block that is not running text, and a paragraph, bullet list, callout or quote moves to the panel beside it. A page that opened with a one-sentence introduction used to give that sentence the wide column and squeeze the drawing it introduced into the narrow panel, where English rings could not fit and the page moved to a plainer layout. Pages whose blocks are all running text keep their order.

A business model canvas measures each block title in the typeface it draws it in, so a block no longer carries an empty title line, or drops its last item, where an English title wraps differently in the heading typeface than in the width estimate. A positioning map places an English name by its real width instead of half again, so a crowded map no longer leaves off a name that fits. A waterfall with a value or running total past 1e300, which used to draw every bar at NaN, is refused by validate with the same advice charts give: divide every item by one power of ten and name the unit in `unit`.
