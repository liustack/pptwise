A fact page set as one large figure (the scoreboard page on arena, heritage, ink, journal, lecture, luxe, museum, playbill, stage, terminal and thesis) no longer leaves out what it has no place for. When the figure comes from a `kpi_cards`, a heading over it, the figure's icon or delta arrow, or a subheading beside a cited source now hands the page to the plain layout, which draws all of it. Before, they were left off the slide with nothing to say so. To keep the large figure on a page with a heading, write the figure itself as the heading, its label as the subheading and its source as the footnote.
