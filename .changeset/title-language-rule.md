Built-in titles now follow a Chinese-specific rule. An item counts as Chinese when it has Han characters, no Japanese kana or Korean hangul, and at least as many Han characters as Latin words, and a component switches to its Chinese titles when more than half of its items do. English content that names a place in Chinese, and Japanese or Korean content, keep the English titles. PEST quadrants (政治 / 经济 / 社会 / 技术), the business model canvas blocks, and a waterfall's automatic total (合计) now follow the same rule as SWOT and the five forces.
