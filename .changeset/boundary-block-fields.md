When a cover, chapter or ending face cannot draw a block because its items carry fields the face has no place for, `validate` now says which: what the face draws of each item, the fields to leave out, and which items carry them. A row card's text on rally's pill row, a milestone's description on rally's or ember's closing steps, an icon on a ticker figure, a picture with no caption in runway's line-up and the like used to get a refusal that said only that the face could not draw the block as written.
