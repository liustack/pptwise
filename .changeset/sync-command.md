`pptwise sync` installs or updates the content packs your license covers, the same as `pptwise packs sync`. The top-level help now shows `login`, `sync`, and `logout` for content packs. `license set/status/clear` and `packs sync/list` keep working and leave the help. Every hint that said `pptwise packs sync` now says `pptwise sync`, and a hint for a missing license points at `pptwise login <key>`.
