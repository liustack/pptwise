Sixteen components and one face now keep ordinary-length English and mixed-language text whole in narrow columns instead of cutting it at the end of a line. Chevron process notes, five forces items, hub and spoke descriptions, KPI card and progress donut sources, matrix cell titles, sankey node labels, swimlane lane names and step descriptions wrap onto further lines, and the drawing grows to hold them. A comparison table whose proportional column split would cut a header or a cell sizes its columns from what each one needs and wraps the widest column. A cycle node whose label will not fit its circle, such as a single long English word, stretches sideways into a capsule of the same height. Decision tree branch cards grow to the height of the outcomes they lead to, conditions may run back across the column they come from, and outcome names share one size that shrinks as far as the 16px floor when one of them needs it. Icon cards shrink their icon node, numbered cards close the gaps between pills, and a staircase unit takes the room its number leaves before any text is cut. Icon cards also size their type against the number of lines their titles really take, so a tall card row, like the one the split band face draws, no longer cuts each description a line short. Timeline end labels wrap short of their neighbour instead of running under it. The Runway spotlight face wraps a long heading onto two lines, and its fallback heading is fitted to the full line it is set on. Pages whose text already fit render exactly as before.
