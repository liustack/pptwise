A bar or stacked chart's point can carry `status: "estimate"` for a past value nobody published that was worked out from figures that were, such as a year read back from "1.8 points lower than in 2018". The bar is drawn pale inside a heavier dashed outline, and a series that mixes estimates with reported values gets an Estimate entry in its legend.
