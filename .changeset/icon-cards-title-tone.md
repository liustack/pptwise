`icon_cards` can carry a short title over the cards (`title`) and say what kind of news a card is (`items[].tone`). The ordinary cards set the title over them as a table does and draw a toned card's icon in the theme's ink for that news; a bento grid keeps a titled set whole and draws a toned tile's icon the same way. The hand-set compositions leave such cards to the ordinary ones.
