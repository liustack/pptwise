A picture's caption paints a `**…**` run as a marked run on the photo pages that take the whole page (`image-split`, `image-top`, `image-bottom`, `image-annotate` and the grid and notice photo pages) and on the ordinary `image`. They fitted the caption as raw text and printed its asterisks. A caption with no mark draws as before.
