A tag can say what kind of news it is: `tone` is `danger`, `warning` or `success`, the same words `timeline`, `kpi_cards` and `row_cards` already use. The ordinary renderers outline the tag in the theme's ink for that news, so 「超说明书」 or "Off-label" reads as a breach beside tags that only name a row.
