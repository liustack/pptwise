A bar on its side, or a part of a share bar, can carry a `note`: a few words printed after its value, after a middle dot, such as 基准线, 低约 37% or 62.36 元. The ordinary chart prints it with the value, and a share bar's part takes it inside or over the bar with its value. Any other chart refuses it. The shared hand-set plots and ledger's figure page leave such a chart to the ordinary one.
