New component `sketch`: a schematic, with no figures, of how a study tells its effect apart. `"kind": "discontinuity"` draws an outcome jumping at a cutoff, two fitted lines over scattered points with the jump's arrow and its name. `"kind": "difference_in_differences"` draws a treated group's trend leaving its control's after an event, the path it would have kept dashed and both groups named. The IR now has 63 components.
