A bar chart can carry a `reference`: one value drawn as a dashed line across the bars, such as a benchmark or a threshold (`{ "value": 1.37, "label": "EU benchmark 1.370" }`). The ordinary chart draws it over the bars, upright or on their side, names it in its legend with a dashed swatch, and grows the value axis to hold it. Any other chart type refuses it. The shared hand-set plots and ledger's figure page leave such a chart to the ordinary one.
