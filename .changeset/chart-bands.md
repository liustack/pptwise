A chart can mark a value range across its plot with `bands`, such as a target range a line should stay in: `[{ "from": 4.5, "to": 5, "label": "Target range" }]`. The range is tinted across the plot behind the data with its label inside it, and the value axis grows to hold it. Line, area and upright bar charts take up to two. A theme's hand-set plot with no value axis leaves such a chart to the ordinary chart.
