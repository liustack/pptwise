The homeroom theme's two faint notebook rules on content pages now sit at the bottom of the page, below the footer line, instead of across the middle of the content area. Before, they ran through chart axis labels, card text, table rows and other words that landed at those heights, and read like a strikethrough. Cover and chapter pages keep their rules where they were. When a deck turns on the content-page footer or logo (`branding` set to `full` or `minimal`), the footer area belongs to that, and content pages draw no rules.
