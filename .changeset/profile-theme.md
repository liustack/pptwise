A new built-in theme, `profile`, sets a candidacy, a self-introduction or a career plan as a magazine profile of the speaker: white paper, claims, names and figures in a serif, open columns under hairlines, a photograph bled off one side, and one accent a page on what the page is about. Each content page opens on a short line naming the part of the talk, numbered from the deck's `course.stages` and the page's `stage`. Its cover sets the speaker's name large beside their portrait, with the occasion, the date, the post held and the post wanted, and three figures. Its chapter sets the part's title in the serif on white paper. Its ending sets the closing promise, a line inviting questions, and the speaker's card with a round avatar drawn as a native ellipse crop. Every content page's footer carries the deck's label, the speaker's name and the page number. `pptwise themes` now lists 23 presets, and `career` joins the occasions a theme can serve.
