Two layouts no built-in theme uses, the Crayon Triptych list page and the Strip Grid photo page, set a page's subheading and its footnote on one closing line and printed only the subheading when a page wrote both. The footnote now follows the subheading on that line, after a middle dot, the way the statement pages join a quote's speaker and a page's footnote.
