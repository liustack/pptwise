On a mid-tone page `background`, about as light as `#777777` or `#6B7B8C`, text the theme's own ink cannot carry is now set in pure black, which reaches the 4.5:1 body text needs, where it used to get white or near-black and miss it on most themes. A probe of points, data and statement pages painted `#777777` drew `low-contrast` on 16 of the 18 themes that offer those kinds and now on 5, all of them KPI labels on a card shaded from the background. Grounds where the old ink already read keep it. The `low-contrast` message now tells the author to move the background or bind another theme, since the text color is the theme's.
