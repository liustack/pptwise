Lecture has a new look, a night class at a green board. Every page stands on a blackboard in a wooden frame with a chalk ledge along its foot (a white and a yellow piece of chalk and an eraser), the deck's organization and label written on the ledge and the page's count 「3 / 18」 at the top right, its number PowerPoint's slide-number field. Every content page uses `chalkboard-sheet`: the page's `kicker` as the lesson's step small at the top left, the title in the heading serif across the whole measure, and the body drawn in the `chalkboard` setting, with an example's `stamp` (「例题 · 数字为虚构」) where the page draws it. The cover uses `chalkboard-cover`, the topic in yellow chalk with one stroke under it and what the class promises. A chapter uses `chalkboard-chapter`, the part's name with a stroke of yellow chalk under it. The ending uses `chalkboard-ending`: the homework with a box to tick beside each task, the next date in yellow and the dismissal, over the page's photograph. Lecture's headings now set their Latin in Times New Roman over SimSun, so an English title and its curly quotes are no longer set full width. If you copied lecture into your own theme file, its menu still names the old faces and keeps working as before.
