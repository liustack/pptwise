A figure the author marks (`**…**` around a `kpi_cards` value) now stands out in `bento-panel`: it takes the theme's emphasis ink and the other figures in its group take the text ink, as they do in the ordinary kpi cards. Every figure in a bento group used to take the accent, so the mark had nothing to stand out from. Groups with no marked figure keep the accent.
