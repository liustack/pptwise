The shared compositions take a seventh setting, `memo`: a typed memorandum on paper. Clauses are numbered 「一、」 in the deck's own numerals beside their titles in the heading face, tables are open under a 2px rule of ink with each row's kind of source in its own outlined tag, labels, dates and sums are typed in mono, and the row a page lands on sits on a pale tint of the mark. The setting also brings the memo's paper pieces any face can use: a photograph pasted in as a turned exhibit with its typed caption (「附图 1 · …」), a stamp with its date, and a margin label beside a heading.
