A waterfall's falling bars and totals now show on themes whose primary is a dark ground for white type rather than an ink (luxe, ledger, rally, terminal, museum, stage and lecture). They used to be painted in that primary, a step off the page, and all but vanished. They are now lifted toward the readable ink until they read at 3:1, a lighter shade of the same colour, and every other theme keeps its bars as they were.
