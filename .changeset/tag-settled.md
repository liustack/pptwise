A tag can say its verdict is final: `settled: true` on 纳入, 不纳入 or "Approved", against an open or conditional one such as 暂缓 or "Case by case". The ordinary renderers fill a settled tag instead of outlining it, in the emphasis colour like the marked row's tag, or in a pale grey with muted words when it is also `quiet`, a final no.
