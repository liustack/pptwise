A page can carry a `ballot`: the boxes a committee ticks beside each of the page's items (`choices`, two to four, such as 同意、不同意、弃权 or For, Against, Abstain) and an optional line left blank to sign (`signature`, its label). Only a face that declares a place for it draws it, and validate refuses it on any other, naming the face. Page files may fill it.
