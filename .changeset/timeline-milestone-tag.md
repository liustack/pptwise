A timeline milestone can carry a `tag`, a few words that say where it stands (such as 已定 or Proposal, with a `basis` to say how firm it is), and a `source`, where its date or rule comes from (such as the act that sets it). The ordinary timeline sets the source in a small line under the milestone's words and the tag under that, in either layout. The shared hand-set timeline compositions leave such a page to the ordinary timeline, and terminal's checklist close declares what it has no place for.
