Two new content faces and a new `image-split` column, drawn from ledger's 2026-10 board. `panel-sheet` sets a page's evidence in dark panels, each named in a small title bar with its unit on the right: a records table, an options table, a chart beside the author's figures, a row of figures, a timeline in one or two lanes, columns or bars. `panel-figure` sets one figure at 200px with a comparison of bars or a few more figures beside it. The `panel` column of `image-split` lays the photograph down the left edge to edge, with the claim and a ledger of figures beside it and the photograph's caption over the source. A theme names them in its menu like any other face.
