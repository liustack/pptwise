A tag can say what kind of source it names: `evidence` is `trial`, `label`, `company`, `press`, `draft`, `official` or `registry`. The tag is outlined in the ink the theme keeps for that kind (a journal's trial and an official document in the primary, a label and a registry in a quieter series colour, a draft in the next, a company's own figures in the warning ink, a press report in the muted ink), so a reader tells a journal's figure from a company's at a glance. The ordinary `kpi_cards`, `data_table`, `comparison` and `icon_cards` draw it that way. A theme that sets its tags in its own inks keeps them.
