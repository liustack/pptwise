A share bar whose parts are percentages of a whole of 100% states the marked run's total once. 「肥胖和超重 50.7%」 used to read 「肥胖和超重 50.7%，占 50.7%」, and "Obese and Overweight 50.7%, 50.7%" in English. Parts in any other unit, or percentages that do not make up the whole, still give their share after the total.
