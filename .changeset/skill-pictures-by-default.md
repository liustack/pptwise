The skill now gives a deck pictures without being asked: the cover, where its theme draws over a photograph, and every `photo` page, other pages only where the picture is the point. The model reads the frame from `asset-brief`, searches the free stock libraries, looks at the numbered contact sheet and pins the one that fits. It needs no key: with none set, search uses Openverse, and a Pexels or Pixabay key only improves the results. Say you want no pictures and it adds none.
