A `gantt` can mark up to two single moments across its bars (`milestones`), such as a check the plan turns on, and a bar can say its stretch is not settled (`items[].basis`). The ordinary gantt draws a moment as a line down the rows with a diamond and its label under them, and an unsettled bar as a dashed outline. A blank axis label leaves its tick unnamed and lends its room to the label before it, so a 20-month axis can name every third month whole.
