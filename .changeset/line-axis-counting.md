A line or area chart whose categories count evenly (2015, 2016, … or 10, 20, 30) names every second or third point when the labels do not fit the room between points even at the floor size, instead of cutting every label to a stub. A half-width chart of the years 2015 to 2025 printed 「201 201 201」 and now names 2015, 2017 and so on to 2025. Categories that do not count keep a label each, cut and marked as before.
