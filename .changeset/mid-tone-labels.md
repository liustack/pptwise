On a mid-tone page background such as `#777777`, an iceberg's waterline name and a row card's description now read at 4.5:1. The waterline name takes its ink from the page it stands on rather than the water under the line, so on ember and homeroom it keeps the theme's own colour. A row card's description stays dimmed wherever the card lets it read that way, and goes to full strength where it does not.
