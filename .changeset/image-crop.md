`image` and `image_grid` items take a `crop`: the part of the picture to keep when it is cropped to its frame, `[left, top, width, height]` as fractions of the whole picture. One group photograph can now show each of its people in a frame of their own, and a cover photograph can keep a head that sits above the middle. The preview cuts the picture with a clip of one rectangle and PowerPoint crops it the same way. The ordinary picture and grid keep the part. A hand-set composition that does not steps aside for them, and a cover or chapter face that frames its own photograph centres it as before.
