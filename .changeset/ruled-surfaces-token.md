A theme can set its shared components as print with `shape.surfaces: "ruled"`. Cards lose their ground and rounded corners and are held by hairlines and white space, a number is set as the figure itself rather than in a disc, a solid block becomes an outline or a rule, a table runs on rules with no tinted row, and what a page leads with is set in weight and ink under one 2px accent rule. Charts keep their marks. A theme that leaves the token out renders exactly as before.
