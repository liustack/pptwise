A timeline can divide its axis into up to three named spans: `periods`, each a `from`, a `to` (written the way the milestones write their dates) and a `label`, with an optional `basis` such as `proposal` for a span that exists only if a proposal passes. The ordinary timeline names every span in a row under its milestones, a dashed swatch for a span that is not settled. A vertical timeline refuses them. The shared hand-set timeline compositions leave such a page to the ordinary timeline, and terminal's checklist close declares the spans it has no place for.
