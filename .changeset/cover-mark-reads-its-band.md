A cover's top-left mark (a legal classification, or the confidentiality mark) takes its color from the band of the cover's own it stands on, not from the page's background. On runway's cover painted `#777777` the mark came out black on the cover's near-black stage at 1.14:1, and on homeroom's cover painted a mid-tone it read 2.92:1 on the board. A classification on five more covers that paint their primary under the corner (`fashion-masthead`, `header-band`, `left-anchor`, `show-headline`, `split-diagonal`) was set in the page's dark ink on that primary. Each now reads. A mark on a cover that paints nothing under it is unchanged.
