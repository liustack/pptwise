A positioning map keeps a subject's name at least 8px above or below any other name or axis label, so two names stacked a line apart no longer read as one name on two lines. A crowded corner tries a few more spots before any name is left off.

A word cloud, a Venn diagram's labels and a harvey-balls legend size Chinese text, and English text in Georgia or Microsoft YaHei, at the width it really paints instead of half again. A cloud on a small panel keeps every word where it used to shrink its sizes or leave some out, and a Venn label in SimSun or KaiTi gets a pill that fits it.

A business model canvas on a narrow content area gives a long item as many lines as it needs instead of cutting it after three. Where a block title such as "Customer Relationships" has a word wider than its column, the canvas moves to a wider layout instead of printing a cut title. A Chinese block title wraps between its characters, so it keeps the canvas on the page at widths where it needs two lines.
