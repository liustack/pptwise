proposal now reads as a printed proposal rather than a web page.

- No index tabs down the right edge. Content pages and the close wear a running head: the deck's label at the left, the page's section at the right, its label in `course.stages` with no number (「算账」, "Numbers"), a hairline under both. The source sits at the foot's left beside the page number.
- A section page is petrol on its left half beside its photograph on the right, with the photograph's caption from the page's `footnote`. Its `stage` names the part under the number when the `kicker` has no 「 · 」.
- Cards, chips and buttons gave way to hairlines and ruled tables. A row's kind of source is a column of its own, the figure a page lands on is set in the brick red, never on a block of it.
- The cards of the shared components (`kpi_cards`, `numbered_cards`, `row_cards` and the like) are no longer warm sand: white like the paper, framed by a hairline, square cornered.
- The close sets each decision on a ruled row with square boxes to tick. Its `paragraph` is a line to fill in, each run of 「＿」 a line to write on (「约踏勘时间 ＿＿＿ 年 ＿＿ 月 ＿＿ 日」).
