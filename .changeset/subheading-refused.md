A subheading a page's layout has no place for is now refused by validate instead of disappearing from the slide. Seven layouts in the built-in themes never drew one, or drew it only when nothing else took its line: the statement page on almanac, ink, journal, ledger, luxe, memo, terminal, thesis and vermilion and brief's statement page set it as the line under the claim only when the page has no paragraph or quote, and rally's statement page, the quotation pages on journal and ink, ember's chapter and the thesis ending have no place for one at all. Each refusal names the layout and says where the words can go, and on a cover, chapter or ending it points at the spec page's `summary`, which is where a deck project's subheading comes from. A deck that passed before and loses nothing still passes. The two shipped examples on thesis dropped their ending's subheading, which never reached the slide.
