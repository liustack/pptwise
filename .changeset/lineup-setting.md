The shared compositions gain a `lineup` setting: a fashion show's running order, the words in the ink of the type, hairlines between rows and one drop of crimson a page. Eleven compositions come with it: `order` (the parts of a review as exit numbers), `standfirst` (a claim set large over two to four figures), `duet` (two figures never added), `collage` (a moodboard of six pictures), `thread` (steps along one hairline over their photographs), `lengths` (figures of one unit drawn as lines to scale, one of them carrying a `delta`), `shades` (a picture bracketed into grades), `atelier` (a sample beside the ways it was made), `parade` (looks in a row of tall windows), `look` (one look on a page of its own, its number read off an `insight_panel` title such as "LOOK 01") and `bounds` (what a piece of work did and did not do). They keep the part of a picture an author crops to. Any face can offer them; runway's content pages use them.
