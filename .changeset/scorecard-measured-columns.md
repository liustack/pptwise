A scorecard whose gap or verdict is longer than its column no longer disappears from the page. When the fixed column widths would cut a cell, the card measures its columns from its own words, and when one line each is still too wide, the goal, target and gap columns wrap to two lines and the rows grow to hold them. Only a card too wide even then is declared as dropped.
