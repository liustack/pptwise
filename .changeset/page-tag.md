A page can carry a `tag` set with its heading, saying what the whole page rests on, such as 「RCT · NEJM 2025 · 751 例」 or "Draft for comment, June 2026", with an optional `evidence` kind that colours it by its source. Like `kicker`, `fields` and `stamp`, only a face that declares a place for it draws it, and validate refuses it on any other, naming the face. Page files may fill it.
