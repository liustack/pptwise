## ember, low-altitude delivery pitch sample, 2026-10

Settled on the compliance page (p12): see the board in [compositions/locks](../../compositions/locks/ember.board.png). The round's decisions are in [rounds/2026-10-06-ember](../../rounds/2026-10-06-ember/README.md).

**What it looks like.** In the ordinary chevrons a stage's `icon` sits at the top right of its text, on the line of its number. `locks` sets it at the right of each gate's band, beside 「第 1 关」 ("Gate 1").

**Why.** Five gates read as five different permissions when each has its own symbol: a fingerprint for registration, a licence card, an umbrella for cover.

**What it gave up.** Nothing on stages without an icon. The ordinary chevrons keep their geometry.
