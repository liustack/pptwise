# callout

`title` and `tag`: a callout with a bold title over its text, and a small tag that says what it rests on.

Code: [`src/components/callout.tsx`](../../../src/components/callout.tsx), schema in [`src/ir/components/callout.ts`](../../../src/ir/components/callout.ts).

## almanac, CBAM sample, 2026-10

Settled on the timeline page (p03), the actual-values page (p10) and the routes page (p14): see the boards in [compositions/calendar](../../compositions/calendar/almanac.board.png), [compositions/paired](../../compositions/paired/almanac.board.png) and [compositions/survey](../../compositions/survey/almanac.board.png). The round's decisions are in [rounds/2026-10-05-almanac](../../rounds/2026-10-05-almanac/README.md).

**What it looks like.** The `title` bold over the text, the icon beside the title when there is one. The `tag` under the text at the ordinary tag's size, in the shared form (`basis` and `evidence` included): 「本企业实测：待核查后填入」 dashed, 「企业口径 · 据报道」 in the quiet ink. In the yearbook setting the compositions that take a callout set it on a card edged in its tag's ink.

**Why.** A note that warns how far a figure can be trusted says what it is about in a few words, and says what it rests on in the same form as every other figure.

**What it gave up.** One tag a callout. A tag wider than the text's measure is declared, never squeezed.
