**What it looks like.** A photograph 420 by 300 in a sky-blue frame with its note under it. Beside it a purple card drawn by hand a study, its symbol and who was studied (16px), what was found (14/23) and where it was published in the grey, and under the cards a caution in the grey. At the right what to do: its lead line (the `paragraph` before the list, 20px) with a stroke of the section's crayon under it, then a hand-drawn box a thing, ticked in the leaf green.

**Why.** Settling in is anxious ground, and putting what to do beside the studies says the advice rests on evidence without overselling it.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes an `image`, an untitled `icon_cards` of one or two studies with symbols and a tag with words alone, optionally a `callout` with words alone, then a one-line `paragraph` and a checklist `bullets` of two to five.
- A card with a tone, a bullets of another style, or a study, caution, lead or thing past its room send the page back.
