**What it looks like.** On the left the background in two to four rows, each its icon in the mark, a bold title at 21px and a muted line at 15px under it, a hairline between rows. On the right a card filled with the mark: the panel's icon and title tracked in a pale ink, then each ask numbered large in the mono face, its title bold in the readable ink and a line under it, a pale hairline between asks.

**Why.** A briefing that asks for a decision says so on its first page, and sets the reasons beside the ask rather than above it, so the committee reads both at once.

**What it gave up.**

- A `row_cards` of two to four items with no highlight, tone or sub line, then an `insight_panel` of one to three rows and no footnote.
- A title past one line, a row's text past two lines, an ask's title past one line or its text past two sends the page back.
