**What it looks like.** Panels three to a row on the stage's dark, each outlined by a hairline: the measure's icon (the first in the magenta, the rest grey) and its name, then three short bars in the dim violet where its figure will stand, the line that says when its target is set (the card's tag, 「目标：首站复盘后定」, in the ink of its tone), and how the measure is counted in grey. On the band's floor a bold closing line.

**Why.** The proposal promises measures, not results. Empty slots say plainly that the targets wait for the first stop, while the panels say what will be counted and how.

**What it gave up.**

- The three bars are the empty slot of a scoreboard, not a figure: every card's tag must say so (`tone: "warning"` or `basis: "pending"`).
- An `icon_cards` of three to six, each with such a tag; then optionally a `callout` with no title, icon or tag.
- The closing line sits on the band's floor, y624 on a page without a source and clear of the source on a page with one.
- A name past one line, a tag's line past one line, how it is counted past two lines or the closing line past one line sends the page back.
