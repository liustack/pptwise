**What it looks like.** One line 4px in the paper white across x120 to x1100 over a faint silver fill to the baseline on y560, the value scale a round ceiling over the largest value. Only the ends are labelled: each end's value bold at 34px with its year at 14px over it, the first in the paper white, the last in silver. The point the author noted is a silver ring, and the run of falls that ends on it a faint silver band from the last high, its note over the band bold at 15px in silver. The years at 12px in the dim along the foot. At the bottom left the unit and the series at 14px in the sand (「单位：亿美元，自研游戏海外实际销售收入」), at the bottom right the one figure that sums the line up at 16px (「3.85 倍　年均约 14.4%」). The source under them.

**Why.** Ten years told as one gesture: where it started, where it is, and the two years it fell.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes a `line` chart of one series of 3 to 16 positive values with at most one `note`, and a `kpi_cards` of one.
- The unit and the series stand where the board wrote them, composed in the deck's language (`单位：` in Chinese, 「series, unit」 in English).
