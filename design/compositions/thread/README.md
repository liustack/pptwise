**What it looks like.** The claim over the page. A black line across the page with a dot where each step begins. Under each dot its number at 40px in the serif, its name at 17px in bold and what it involves in two short lines at 12/20 in the stone grey. Under the steps the photographs of the work stand each under the step it shows, centred under the middle steps when there are fewer pictures than steps, each with its caption small and grey under it.

**Why.** A process is a line the work moves along. The photographs anchor the steps that need showing.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes a `steps` of three to five items with no icon or tone, then optionally an `image_grid` of up to as many pictures as steps, with no icon or tag, not led by one.
- A name past one line, a line past two, or a caption wider than its picture sends the page back.
