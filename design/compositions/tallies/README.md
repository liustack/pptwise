# tallies

The reasons a decision rests on, each with the figure behind it: an icon, a name, the figure large in the mark, and the sentence that says where it comes from.

Code: [`src/layouts/compositions/tallies.tsx`](../../../src/layouts/compositions/tallies.tsx).

## memo, four-day week decision sample, 2026-10

Settled on the reasons page (p03): see the board in [compositions/annex](../annex/memo-p03.board.png). The round's decisions are in [rounds/2026-10-05-memo](../../rounds/2026-10-05-memo/README.md).

**What it looks like.** One row a reason, up to 146px apart on hairlines: the icon at 28px in the mark, the name bold in the heading face at 22px, the figure under it at 48px in the heading face in the mark in a 160px column, and the note beside the figure at 16/26, up to three lines.

**Why.** On this page the figures are the reasons, so every one is in the mark and the name says which is which.

**What it gave up.**

- Two to four items, each with an icon, a label and a note, and no delta, tag or source. A figure wider than 160px sends the page back.
