The board in the table is the first round's. The print round set this page by the redrawn board's rules, and the engine render shows the page as it is set now.

**What it looks like.** Two columns of rows parted by hairlines, each a paper: an empty square box to tick, its icon in petrol, its name bold, what it is used for in grey, and who holds it in small grey at the row's right. Under the rows, the closing line under a 2px rule of petrol, its first sentence bold and the rest grey.

**Why.** The proposal's figures become the client's own once the client's papers arrive. A box per paper and the department that holds it make the request easy to hand on.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes an `icon_cards` of two to six with no title or tone, each with a tag, then optionally a `callout` with no title, icon or tag.
- A name past one line beside its tag, a text past one line, or a closing line past one line sends the page back.
