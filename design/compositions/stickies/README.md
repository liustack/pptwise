**What it looks like.** The claim over the page. Under it the notes in rows of three (two when there are four), 340 by 196, each a pale crayon, turned a degree or so this way or that with a solid shadow under it and a strip of half-clear tape across its top. On each, upright, the rule's symbol (28px) and name (22/34), what it says (15/24, two lines), and where it is written (the card's `tag`) on a white label.

**Why.** A law read to parents is a handful of things to remember, and a note on the wall says each is one to keep.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes an untitled `icon_cards` of three to six, every card with a symbol, a title and words, a tag with words alone.
- A card with a tone, a name past one line, words past two lines or a label wider than the note send the page back.
- Only the note and its tape are turned. The words stay upright, so the export keeps them as plain text.
