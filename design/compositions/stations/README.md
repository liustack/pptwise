**What it looks like.** A photograph runs across the top of the page, 210px tall. Under it the section line, the claim, and the posts in equal columns, each under a hairline of the ink: the date in the serif in the grey, the post bold in the serif at 22px, the result as a figure bold at 48px with its unit, and the milestone's words under it. The post the speaker holds now hangs from a 3px rule of the accent, its figure in the accent and its tag (「现在」) at the right of its date.

**Why.** A panel remembers a career by what each step left behind. One checkable figure a post turns a list of job titles into a record.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes an `image` and a horizontal `timeline` of two to five milestones, every one with a title and words that hold a number, at most one highlighted and only that one tagged.
- The figure is the last number in the words with its unit, so a milestone whose words hold no number sends the page back.
- A timeline title, lanes, periods, a status or a milestone's source sends the page back.
