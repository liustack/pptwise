**What it looks like.** Under the section line the claim set large at 48/67 heavy across the measure, a run the author marked underlined in red. Under it the paragraph that says it in full at 19/34. From y470, under a 2px rule of the ink, the figures in equal columns parted by pale hairlines: each figure at 60px in the figure face with its unit after it, the label at 15px, and its legend mark (「达成」, 「我牵头」, 「我的失误」). A figure marked whole is in the accent, a negative one in red.

**Why.** A reviewer who reads one page should leave with the verdict: how much, how much of it was the speaker's, and the one miss.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes a `paragraph` and a `kpi_cards` of two to four.
- A paragraph past four lines or a figure wider than its column sends the page back.
