The board in the table is the first round's. The print round set this page by the redrawn board's rules, and the engine render shows the page as it is set now.

**What it looks like.** One ruled table: its column headers in small grey over a 2px ink rule, then each table a group: its title as a small tracked line in petrol, its rows under it parted by hairlines, each with its icon in petrol, its item bold, what it covers, how it is priced bold and its amount. An amount still to be set (「— — —」) is a blank to fill in a grey with air between the dashes. The row the page leads with has its icon and its tag, bold after what it covers, in the brick red, on the paper like the rest. A 2px ink rule under the last row.

**Why.** A quote before a survey cannot have prices. Listing every line by the industry's own cost breakdown, with the blanks left visibly blank, shows what the price will be made of and that nothing is hidden.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes two to four `data_table`s, each with a title, the same three or four columns and one to five rows, each row with an icon, a tag only on the one row marked `highlight`, eight rows at most in all.
- The blanks are a grey that reads at 4.5:1. The board's paler dashes read at 2:1.
- Tables whose columns differ, a cell past its column, a tag past the column of what its row covers, a group title past one line or a table taller than the band sends the page back.
