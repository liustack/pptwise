**What it looks like.** The photograph bleeds from the page's left edge to x560 the full height of the page. In the column from x640: the section line and the claim, then the page's `icon_cards` as open rows, the first under a hairline of the ink and the rest under pale ones. Each row: its icon at 28px in the accent, its title bold in the serif at 22px, its words at 16px in the body ink. The footer row moves into the column, and the photograph's credit follows the deck's label there.

**Why.** A panel first wants to know what kind of person stands in front of them. Three plain traits, each with the evidence behind it, beside a picture of the speaker at work, say it faster than a list of duties.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes an `image` and an `icon_cards` of two to four, every item with a title and no tag.
- A row past two lines, rows that run into the footer, or a claim past two lines in the column sends the page back.
