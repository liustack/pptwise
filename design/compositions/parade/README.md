The looks in the order they walk, a row of tall windows.

Code: [`src/layouts/compositions/parade.tsx`](../../../src/layouts/compositions/parade.tsx). The header comment there is the contract. The lineup setting only.

## runway, graduation collection review sample, 2026-10

Settled on p12, the chapter page that opens the third part: its board and engine render are in [faces/lineup-chapter](../../faces/lineup-chapter/README.md). The round's decisions are in [2026-10-08-runway](../../rounds/2026-10-08-runway/README.md).

**What it looks like.** A row of tall windows side by side, one a look, each its picture cropped to one figure (a picture's `crop` picks the figure out of a group photograph), and under each its label at 18px in the serif (「LOOK 01」). The first is crimson when the grid leads with it (`emphasis: "first"`). A chapter face hands it the band under its numeral and title. On a content page it is handed the whole page and sets the claim over the row itself.

**Why.** A collection is seen whole before it is seen look by look.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes an `image_grid` of three to eight captioned pictures with no icon or tag.
- A band narrower than 64px a window or shorter than 240px, or a label wider than its window, sends the page back.
