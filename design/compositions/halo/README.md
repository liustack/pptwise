**What it looks like.** A pool of warm light 380px in radius in the middle of the page. The exhibit's number at 12px in copper tracked 6px on y150, the figure at 170px in copper in the serif with its unit at 52px after it, a short seam on y420, the page's claim at 22px in the serif centred under it as the line the figure answers to, what the figure rests on at 14px in old paper, and the source small at the foot, all centred.

**Why.** The oldest basalt brought back is one fragment and one number: the page gives it the room a single object gets in a darkened hall.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes a `kpi_cards` of one on a page with a claim, so a `fact` page in a deck project can set a figure and say what it means.
- A claim of two lines moves what follows down a line.
