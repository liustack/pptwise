**What it looks like.** A card a hypothesis down the left: its label set large in emerald in the heading serif (「H1」), its direction as a gold icon, its claim in the heading serif, its expected sign as a chip of pale emerald at the right, then what it says and, in the grey, what it rests on. A photograph at the right with its plain caption, and a closing line with a gold bar under the cards.

**Why.** A committee checks a hypothesis by its sign and its basis. Each card puts the two side by side, and the closing line says only the direction is predicted.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes an `icon_cards` of two to four titled 「label：claim」 and tagged with the expected sign, whose text ends with what it rests on, then an `image` and optionally a `callout`.
- A label wider than 80px or a line past its card sends the page back.
