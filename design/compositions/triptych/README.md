# triptych

Two to four pieces of a range shown as a catalogue spread.

Code: [`src/layouts/compositions/triptych.tsx`](../../../src/layouts/compositions/triptych.tsx). The header comment there is the contract. The invitation setting only.

## luxe, gold dealer conference sample, 2026-10

Settled on p14. The round's decisions are in [2026-10-08-luxe](../../rounds/2026-10-08-luxe/README.md).

**What it looks like.** Each piece a photograph taller than wide in a fine gold rule, its name under it in the gold serif tracked wide, and one line on what it is centred in ivory, a figure the author marks set in the serif in the lifted gold, a little larger.

**Why.** A range is shown the way a catalogue shows it: the piece first, its name engraved under it.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes one `image_grid` of two to four photographs, each captioned "name：line" with no icon or tag.
- A caption without its name, or a name or line wider than its photograph, sends the page back.
