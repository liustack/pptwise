The board in the table is the first round's. The print round set this page by the redrawn board's rules, and the engine render shows the page as it is set now.

**What it looks like.** A column a way to pay, the columns under a hairline and parted by hairlines: its name at 21px in petrol, who it suits in small grey and, after a " · ", a part marked whole under it in bold brick red (「贵司出资 0 元」). Then the marked row's label in small grey over its figure at 34px in petrol (28px for a figure of nine characters or more), with the part after a " · " as a line under it. Then each labelled row under a hairline as its label in small grey over its value at 14px bold. Under the columns, the note as up to two lines after its grey icon.

**Why.** Who pays decides who owns the equipment and who keeps the savings. Setting each way in its own column, with the same rows in the same places, lets the finance lead pick by reading across.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes a `comparison` of two to four columns with no title, label column, tags, icons or recommendation, its rows in this order: optionally a row with no label (who each suits), the marked row (the figures), then one to three labelled rows, then optionally a `callout` with no title or tag.
- The " · " is declared, not printed.
- A name past one line, any line past its column, a figure wider than its column or a note past two lines sends the page back.
