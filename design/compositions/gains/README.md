The board in the table is the first round's. The print round set this page by the redrawn board's rules, and the engine render shows the page as it is set now.

**What it looks like.** A line naming the offer with its icon (the page's `verdict_banner`, 「贵司会得到三样东西」), then a column a thing the client gets, the columns under a hairline and parted by hairlines: its icon in petrol, what it is in two lines of 20px bold, its figure at 40px in petrol and a note in two lines of grey. The figure the page lands on (written `**…**`, 「6.1 至 7.7 年」) is in the brick red. Under the columns, one line after a grey icon.

**Why.** A client's management turns to a proposal for what they get. Three things, each with the figure that proves it, answer that before any sum is worked.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes optionally a `verdict_banner`, a `kpi_cards` of two to four with an icon each and no unit, delta, tag, tone or source, at most one value marked whole, then optionally a `callout` with no title or tag.
- A figure wider than its column, a label or a note past two lines, or a banner or closing line past one line sends the page back.
- The board's tangerine figure is brick red, which reads at 5.48 on the paper as it is (the round's decision 15).
