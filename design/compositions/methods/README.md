**What it looks like.** Three photographs 200px tall across the band. Under each a number in a disc of the pen, the method's name bold at 21px and how it goes muted at 15px (the caption written 「名称：做法」), its icon in the mark at the right. Under the row a note on paper of the warning ink's hue at 92% lightness, turned a degree, over a flat shadow: the callout's title and its line, bold at 18px.

**Why.** Methods are easier to remember as pictures, and the one sentence that does the most work belongs on something the room would stick to a monitor.

**What it gave up.**

- An `image_grid` of two to four photographs, each with a caption, then optionally a `callout` with no tag.
- Photographs are cropped square at their corners.
- A note whose title sits over two lines of words moves up just far enough to stay in the band.
