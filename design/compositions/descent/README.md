**What it looks like.** The line leaves its first point, a solid gold dot, and comes to rest at its last, a hollow one, easing out of the one and into the other. The first value in the serif lifted toward the ivory with what it is and when over it, the last in ivory with what it is and when under it. A level the line is read against (a second series that holds one value throughout) is a dashed line in the dim gold across the plot with its name and value at its right end, the line's own name small at the left.

**Why.** A fall from a record is one movement. Drawn as one line it reads at a glance.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes a `line` chart whose first series runs over two to eight categories, its two ends optionally noted, and optionally a second series that holds one value, in a band another composition hands on or on a page of its own.
- A note on a middle point, a second series that moves, or a value past its room sends the page back.
