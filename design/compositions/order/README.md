**What it looks like.** The claim over the page. A column a part, side by side across the measure: a black rule over each, its number at 96px in the serif like an exit number (01 to 06), its symbol, its name at 24px in the serif and a line or two on it at 13/22 in the stone grey. The part the author marks (`emphasis`) has its number in crimson. No cards.

**Why.** The first content page tells the room how the review will walk: five parts, numbered as the exits of a show, the one the review lands on in crimson.

**What it gave up.**

- Takes a `numbered_cards` of three to six items, every one with a symbol or none, no `sub`, at most one marked.
- A name past one line or a line past two sends the page back.
