## runway, graduation collection review sample, 2026-10

**What it looks like.** The page's photograph (its first `image`, with its `crop`) across the whole page, fading to black at its left (82% at the edge, 35% at 46%, clear from 70%). At the top left the page's `kicker` at 11px in bold tracked 8px over a hairline in the paper. The collection's name at 150px in the serif over the picture, one line from 150 down to 96px and otherwise two lines broken at a comma or a colon down to 64px. The subtitle at 17/26. The page's `tag` in crimson at 11px tracked 6px. The page's `footnote` small at the bottom right over a fade of black along the foot. Without a photograph the page is the stage.

**Why.** A collection opens on the work itself, as a magazine opens on its cover look.

**What it gave up.**

- A fade of black along the foot that the board did not draw, so the caption reads on any photograph.
- A name past two lines at 64px is declared dropped rather than cut.
