## profile, membership product manager candidacy sample, 2026-10

The board drew no section page. It is worked out from the section line and the cover. The round's decisions are in [rounds/2026-10-12-profile](../../rounds/2026-10-12-profile/README.md).

**What it looks like.** White paper. From y260 at the left: the section line, the section's title bold in the serif at 56/72, up to three lines, stepping down to 48px before it cuts and says so, and the subheading at 18px in the grey. The page's photograph down the right half from x640 when it has one. The footer row as on a content page.

**Why.** A twelve-page talk rarely needs one, but a longer career plan does, and it should turn the page as quietly as a magazine does.

**What it gave up.**

- No motif. The face draws its own footer row.
