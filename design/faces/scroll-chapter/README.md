**What it looks like.** At the right a tall painting (the page's `background`, at x760, 340px wide and 600px tall) in a 10px mount of the whiter paper with a hairline inside it. Beside it, upright, the hall and the footer's `label` (「文化讲堂」, 「二〇二六年十月」, 14px in the taupe). Left of the painting the volume number stands upright (the page's `kicker`, 「卷之一」, 72px in the heading face in cinnabar, tracked 12px). At the left the volume's name (`heading`) at 52px in the heading face in the ink, the subtitle (`subheading`) under it at 20px in the grey, and under that a single stroke of ink 150px long. At the bottom left the page's `footnote`.

**Why.** A new volume opens on its painting and its number, and the number is the one large cinnabar on the page.

**What it gave up.**

- In a Latin deck the volume number is turned a quarter to read from the top, and a name too long for one line takes two.
- No motif and no footer marks.
