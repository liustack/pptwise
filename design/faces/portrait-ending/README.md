**What it looks like.** A photograph 600px wide down the left. In the column from x680: a 28px rule of the accent, the closing promise bold in the serif at 42px, the line inviting questions (the page's `paragraph`) in the serif at 22px in the accent. Under a hairline of the ink the speaker's card: a round avatar drawn as a native ellipse with the picture cropped into it, the name bold in the serif at 22px, and the post held and wanted (`subheading`) at 14px in the grey. The footer in the column.

**Why.** The panel's questions come next. The speaker's face and name stay on the screen while they ask.

**What it gave up.**

- The avatar is the page's `image`, cropped by the author's `crop` or toward the top of the picture, never a picture cropped beforehand.
- No photograph leaves the left side empty.
