**What it looks like.** The page's `background` photograph across the whole page (`drawsPhoto`), under a gradient of the page colour from the left edge (97%) fading out by x760, so every word stands on the left. A 22px sprout and the page's `kicker` at the top left, 13px bold in the mark with its characters 2px apart. The question bold at 44/60 from y110, under it a 60 by 4 rule in the accent. Then the first `bullets`, up to four, each written 「类别：事项」 and set on a translucent card: an empty box to tick, the kind small and bold with its characters 2px apart, the item bold at 22px in the ink. The item written `**…**` is the one to decide first: its card is edged in the accent and its box and kind take the accent too, the others are edged with a hairline and take the mark. The page's `subheading` (the office and the date) in muted type under the cards. Without a photograph the page takes its colour.

**Why.** A yearbook closes on what the committee is asked to do, each decision a box to tick, the most urgent one marked.

**What it gave up.**

- Only `bullets`, four decisions at most, the cards closing up when they would run past the foot. A kind or an item too long for its card is declared, not cut.
- No folio, as on every theme's ending.
