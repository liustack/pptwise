**What it looks like.** The page's own photograph (confetti falling on a stage) under a darkening of the house colour from the foot (95% at the foot, 60% halfway, 30% at the top). The ticket stub on y64: the page's `kicker` on the stamp (「下一步」, "Next"), the subheading on the stub (「开场前，散场后 · 2027 夏季演唱会季」). The closing line bold at 72/92, its last line on y374. At y456 the page's `timeline` as next steps 340px apart: a dot of a confetti colour, the date at 18px bold, what happens at 15px in the grey, a dotted line between the dots that starts after one date and stops before the next. At y580 a button of the magenta 56px tall with the page's `paragraph` in the dark ink at 20px bold (「拍板，开场」, "Sign off, curtain up").

**Why.** The close asks for the decision that starts the plan. The steps say what happens next month and the button is the decision in the author's own words.

**What it gave up.**

- No button without a `paragraph`: the face never writes its own words on it.
- At most four steps. No motif and no folio.
