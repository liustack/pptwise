The board in the table is the first round's. The print round redrew this page in a design tool outside this folder, and the engine render shows the page as it is set now.

**What it looks like.** At the left a white page. At its top the deck's name (the footer's `label` before its first 「 · 」) at 14px bold in petrol and the date (`meta.date`) at 14px in the grey at the right, over a hairline of the ink. Then who the proposal is for (the page's `kicker`, 「呈 贵司管理层」) at 16px in the grey, its characters 3px apart, the title at 46/60 bold in petrol on one line whenever it fits, a 56 by 3px rule of the brick red under it, and its line at 18px. At the foot, between hairlines, the page's `kpi_cards` as three figures in columns parted by hairlines, each value at 28px bold in petrol (26 or 24px for all three when one does not fit), the one marked whole (`**…**`) in the brick red, its label at 14px and its note at 12px in the grey. At the bottom left the page's `footnote` (「图为 AI 生成的示意图」). At the right, 560px wide and full height, the page's own `background` photograph.

**Why.** A proposal is addressed. The small spaced line says to whom, and the three figures say what they stand to gain before the first page turns.

**What it gave up.**

- Figures with only a value, a unit, a label and a note: an icon, delta, tag, tone or source on a figure, or a fourth figure, declares them dropped.
- No running head, no motif and no folio: the proposal has not been opened yet.
- A title on two lines moves what is under it down a line.
