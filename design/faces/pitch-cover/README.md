**What it looks like.** The page's own `background` photograph fills the page under a darkening of the stage's colour from the foot up (96% at the foot, 70% at 42% of the height, 5% at 75%). At the top left an 88px wedge of the fire, and beside it the occasion and the date (the page's `kicker` and `meta.date`, 「种子轮路演 · 2026 年 10 月」) at 14px bold in the ivory, its characters 4px apart, from x104. The title bold at 64/76, on one line whenever it fits and broken at a comma when it does not, its last line ending at y540. Under it the subheading at 21/32 in the warm grey, and at y612 the page's `bullets` as outlined pills 30px tall, 14px bold in the ivory. Without a photograph the same words stand on the stage, and the wedge stays.

**Why.** A pitch opens on the world the business will fly in, with one line of what it proves and its facts as pills. The fire is the wedge alone: nothing on the cover competes with the title.

**What it gave up.**

- No motif and no running order: the pitch has not started.
- A title that needs two lines breaks after its last clause mark that lets both fit, a comma or a colon ("Drone delivery in cities:" over "medical first"), and where the line runs out only when no clause mark fits.
