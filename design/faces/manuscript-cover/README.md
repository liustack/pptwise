**What it looks like.** At the left a title page on the ivory: the deck's label at the top left (the footer's `label`, 「硕士学位论文开题报告」, 13px bold in the grey, its characters 4px apart) over a gold hairline to x760. The title at 40/60 bold in emerald in the heading serif from y190, broken where the author broke it (a line break in the heading, 「渐进式延迟法定退休年龄」 then 「对 50 至 60 岁城镇职工就业的影响」), or on one line when the author wrote none and it fits. A 60px gold rule 3px thick on y352. The page's `fields`, four at most, as ruled lines from y400: a field's name at 15px bold in the grey, a shorter Chinese name spread to the longest one's width (「日　　期」), its value at 17px bold in the heading serif, a hairline under it. At the foot the page's `footnote` (「图为 AI 生成的示意图」). At the right, 460px wide and full height, the page's own `background` photograph.

**Why.** A proposal defense opens on the title page of the thesis. The fields say what kind of report it is without naming anyone.

**What it gave up.**

- No components: the fields carry the report's facts. A fifth field is declared dropped.
- A title too long for three lines at the measure shrinks toward 30px and is then cut and marked.
- No motif and no folio: the cover draws its own label.
