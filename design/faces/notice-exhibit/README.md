# notice-exhibit

bulletin's evidence page: the notice header, one exhibit on a white card with the place that proves the claim ringed in IKB, and the reading beside it.

Code: [`src/layouts/content-notice-exhibit.tsx`](../../../src/layouts/content-notice-exhibit.tsx). Used by bulletin (evidence).

## bulletin, kinds round, 2026-10

Settled on p05, p06 and p09. The boards and engine renders are in [compositions/proof](../../compositions/proof/README.md). The round's decisions are in [rounds/2026-10-09-bulletin-kinds](../../rounds/2026-10-09-bulletin-kinds/README.md).

**What it looks like.** The notice header, the `proof` composition in the body from y196, the source at the foot. The face numbers the exhibit across the deck: in a Chinese deck figures and tables count apart (「图 2」, 「表 1」), in any other they count together ("Exhibit 3"). An exhibit is the first component of an evidence page, a chart or a table with a title.

**What it gave up.**

- A page with a subheading, or a body `proof` does not take, goes to the notice sheet.
