**What it looks like.** The page's photograph (its first `image`, with its `crop`, or its `background` asset) across the whole page, a fade of the hall from its left (95% at the edge, 70% at 38%, 5% from 70%). The occasion at the top left, 12px in copper tracked 6px (the `kicker`), a 136px copper rule under it on y94. The title at 64/76 in the serif at its regular weight in 720px, on the lines the author broke it into or broken at a comma, its last line ending on y452. Its line at 17/28 in old paper on y470. The date (`meta.date`) at 14px in the serif in the dim tracked 6px on y610. The caption (`footnote`) at the bottom right, 10px in the dim.

**Why.** A science talk in a museum opens like the catalogue of a show: the object first, the title as wall text.

**What it gave up.**

- No motif and no page number.
- With no photograph the page is the hall.
