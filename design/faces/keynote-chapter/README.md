**What it looks like.** The page's photograph across the page under a wash of the black from the left, 90% to 40% at 55% to 20%. The act's number (`kicker`, 「第一章」) at 20px in silver tracked 8px from y220, the name bold at 150/170 from y270, a few points smaller to stay on one line down to 100px, then 96, 80 and 64px on one line or two for an act named in a phrase. The line under it (`subheading`) at 22px in the sand from y460. The clicker along the foot. Without a photograph the page is the black with a faint follow spot.

**Why.** Each act is a new room on the same stage: a picture of where it goes and one word for it.

**What it gave up.**

- The act's number is the author's `kicker`, not counted by the engine, so a talk may call its parts acts, chapters or anything else.
