# marquee-chapter

rally's section page: the section's ticket stub, the title huge, what the section answers in the magenta, and a burst of confetti.

Code: [`src/layouts/chapter-marquee-chapter.tsx`](../../../src/layouts/chapter-marquee-chapter.tsx).

## rally, summer concert season proposal sample, 2026-10

The board drew no section page, and the sample deck has none. The face takes the cover's geometry. The round's decisions are in [rounds/2026-10-06-rally](../../rounds/2026-10-06-rally/README.md).

**What it looks like.** On the house colour, or on the page's own photograph darkened from the foot as on the close, the ticket stub at the top left on y64: the section's number on the magenta stamp, its name (the page's `kicker`) on the stub, numbered with the content pages in the order the names first appear. The title bold at 72/86, its last line on y475; under it the subheading at 26px bold in the magenta, up to two lines; optionally at y576 the page's `row_cards` or `bullets` as pills. Fourteen strips of confetti burst at the top right, from (860, 120) in a box 380 by 260, none on a word.

**Why.** A proposal read section by section needs a page that says the next act has begun, in the same stub every page carries.

**What it gave up.**

- No motif and no folio, as on every theme's section page.
- Pills give way to a subheading that needs two lines.
