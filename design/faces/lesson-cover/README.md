**What it looks like.** The left half, x0 to x720, is the board, the primary darkened, over an 8px ledge of wood. On it: the office (`meta.organization`) and the occasion (`kicker`) on one line, 14px bold in chalk, its characters 3px apart; the title in white at 50/68, on one line if it fits and broken at a comma when it does not, its last line ending at y440; a 180px wavy line of the pen in chalk at y452; the subheading at 19/30 in pale chalk; the page's `bullets` as pills 30px tall with white words and half-white outlines, wrapping to a second row when one cannot hold them; and the deck's `date`. The right half is the page's own `background` photograph, cropped to it. Without a photograph the board fills the page.

**Why.** A class opens on the room it is taught in and the board it is taught from, with how long it runs and how it is checked.

**What it gave up.**

- No motif and no course strip: the class has not started.
- The title breaks at the comma (「在工作中安全、」 over 「有效地用生成式 AI」) where the board's browser broke it where the line ran out.
