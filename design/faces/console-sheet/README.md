terminal's ordinary content page: a crumb in the top left corner, the claim across the full measure over a hairline, the body handed to the shared compositions in the `console` setting, and a mono source line at the foot.

Code: [`src/layouts/content-console-sheet.tsx`](../../../src/layouts/content-console-sheet.tsx), with the frame (`ConsoleCrumb`, `ConsoleHead`, `ConsoleSource`) in [`src/layouts/console-shared.tsx`](../../../src/layouts/console-shared.tsx) and the crumb's words in [`src/layouts/compositions/crumb.tsx`](../../../src/layouts/compositions/crumb.tsx). ## terminal, cloud outage review sample, 2026-10

Settled on every content page. The round's decisions are in [rounds/2026-10-05-terminal](../../rounds/2026-10-05-terminal/README.md). The boards and engine renders are in the composition folders each page links from the round.

**What it looks like.** The crumb at 13px mono at y45: a dot in the mark, the section's number and name in the mark (the chapter heading's words before its colon), a quiet dot and 「P04」 in the muted ink. The page number leaves the crumb when the deck's footer row prints it. The claim bold at 31/42 from x64 across 1152px, at most two lines, its last line on the same baseline whether it takes one line or two, wrapping only when it does not fit. A hairline in the border ink at y156 with a 32 by 3 segment of the mark at its left end. A subheading becomes a standfirst under the rule. The body runs from y180 to y650 and is offered to the compositions in this order: `cards`, `listing`, `log`, `span`, `plates`, `paths`, `screen`, `records`, `table`, `rail`, `waves`. A page none of them takes is drawn by the component renderer in the same band. The source sits at the foot at 12/18 mono after 「src: 」, up to two lines, from y668.

**Why.** An incident console says where you are before it says anything else, and every page of the review reads under the same head. The photo pages are content pages here too: a row of photographs over their figures and a device beside its log are compositions like any other.

**What it gave up.**

- A page the band cannot hold steps aside to the plain sheet, and is declined when that cannot hold it either.
- The motif is off on these pages: the crumb is the page's furniture.
