**What it looks like.** The hall, a pool of warm light 360px in radius at 16% centred at x900, y380. The hall's name (the `kicker`) at the top left over the seam, and again at 14px in copper tracked 10px over the title. The title at 68/96 in the serif at its regular weight, two lines broken at a comma when it does not fit one, the hall's name lifted over it. A 120px copper rule on y416, the line (`subheading`) at 16px in old paper tracked 2px, and the door plate with the page number at the bottom right. Over the page's photograph when it has one, under a veil of the hall from the left, with every word kept.

**Why.** Each part of the talk is a room the audience walks into: a breath, the hall's name, what is inside.

**What it gave up.**

- The page number is drawn by the face, since the shared footer prints it on content pages only.
- A chapter with no `kicker` names no hall: the engine does not invent one.
