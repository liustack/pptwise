crayon's sun and stars in the top right corner, and its folio.

Code: [`src/motifs/motif-crayonbox-motif.tsx`](../../../src/motifs/motif-crayonbox-motif.tsx). The motif paints the footer row itself (`"row"` in [`footer-roles.ts`](../../../src/motifs/footer-roles.ts)).

## crayon, new term parents' meeting sample, 2026-10

Every content page of the board carries it. Redrawn this round (v2): it used to draw a four-ray sun at x1240 and two stars set as text glyphs in a fixed candy pink and purple. The round's decisions are in [2026-10-08-crayon](../../rounds/2026-10-08-crayon/README.md).

**What it looks like.** At the top right a crayon sun in sunny yellow (a ring of radius 14 at x1210 y64 and eight short rays) and two star stickers, tangerine at (1172, 112) and purple at (1222, 118), all in the theme's own colours. On content pages of a deck with a footer: the organization, the label and the notice at the bottom left from y684, 12px in the grey, with the draft and confidentiality marks after them, and the page number in a 36px disc at x1180 y676, its ground the pale tint of the page's section crayon.

**Why.** The sun and the stars are the box's doodles in the corner of every page, and the disc carries the section's colour down to the number.

**What it gave up.**

- The number is PowerPoint's slide-number field.
- A page over a photograph leaves the corner to the picture: no sun and no stars.
- Nothing on the cover, the chapter pages and the close, which draw their own.
