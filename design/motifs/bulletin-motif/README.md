# bulletin-motif

bulletin's mark: three square steps, large to small, top right on every page.

Code: [`src/motifs/motif-bulletin-motif.tsx`](../../../src/motifs/motif-bulletin-motif.tsx).

## bulletin, NEV sample, 2026-10

Every board page carries it. See the top right of [faces/ikb-field-cover](../../faces/ikb-field-cover/bulletin.board.png) for the large white steps and [compositions/rows](../../compositions/rows/bulletin.board.png) for the small IKB ones. The round's decisions are in [rounds/2026-10-03-bulletin](../../rounds/2026-10-03-bulletin/README.md).

**What the board settled.** On content pages, 14, 10 and 7px IKB squares 5px apart, bottom-aligned, from x1158 at y58. On the cover and the ending, the same steps at 44, 30 and 20px, 10px apart, in white, from x1080 at y96. No other decoration on any page.

**Why.** The old motif drew a ruler across the top of every content page and an IKB band behind the title. The steps alone say "bulletin" and leave IKB for the content.

**What it gave up.**

- The ruler and the band are gone from every bulletin page, including the faces this sample does not use.
